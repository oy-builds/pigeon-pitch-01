# Pigeon Pitch — Coo-ber

A city pigeon pitches investors on Coo-ber, itself as a drone-delivery fleet that runs on breadcrumbs and never needs charging. The closing twist turns a pigeon's most notorious city habit into the investor perk: for one seed round, the flock will poop exclusively on competitors' statues.
